Dog Encyclopedia

Three breeds, each with size, personality, and everyday care.

Beagle

  • Size: Small to medium, about 9–14 kg and 33–41 cm tall.
  • Personality: Curious, friendly, and a little nosy — they love following scents.
  • Everyday care: Needs daily walks, a secure fenced yard (they wander!), and short, fun training games.

Golden Retriever

  • Size: Large, about 25–34 kg and 51–61 cm tall.
  • Personality: Gentle, patient, and eager to please — great with people and other pets.
  • Everyday care: Loves swimming and fetch, needs regular brushing (they shed!), and enjoys family activities.

Poodle

  • Size: Comes in standard, miniature, and toy sizes — roughly 2–32 kg depending on type.
  • Personality: Smart, lively, and very trainable; often described as one of the cleverest breeds.
  • Everyday care: Needs regular grooming every 4–6 weeks, daily mental games, and lots of learning tricks.
